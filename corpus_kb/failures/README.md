# Failure And Negative-Knowledge Pages

This page family records repeated validation, conflict, residual, and extractor-failure shapes.

The goal is to make known-dangerous patterns visible to future implementation without turning them into automatic canonical facts.

Pages in this family can feed:

- future KG-bench fixture ideas
- negative-knowledge prior candidates
- targeted rescan design
- debugging and review checklists

They cannot suppress local evidence, erase conflicts, or promote canonical IR truth by themselves.
